Why milk?

For whatever reason, at some point I started using milk and milk based products as my online username for everything. But milk really is great! Milk is one of the most versatile and tasty liquids that humans have discovered. Milk is used to make cheese, chocolate, chowder, and a million other things that start with 'ch-'. And for the vegans reading this, you're not limited to cow's milk either! Almond milk, oat milk, soy milk! All of these are great, non-dairy, vegan friendly things to put in your morning coffee!
